jobhunt

Search thousands of real job openings.
Runs locally. Zero data leaves your machine.

Windows
macOS
Linux

Step 1: Open PowerShell (search "PowerShell" in Start)

Step 2: Paste this and press Enter:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ziad-Abdalla/jobhunt/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex

Step 3: Type jobhunt and press Enter. Your browser opens.

Step 1: Open Terminal (Cmd+Space, type "Terminal")

Step 2: Paste this and press Enter:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ziad-Abdalla/jobhunt/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

Step 3: Type jobhunt and press Enter. Your browser opens.

Step 1: Open a terminal

Step 2: Paste this and press Enter:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ziad-Abdalla/jobhunt/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

pip install jobhunt-app && jobhunt

What you get

  • 17 job boards: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, and 13 more
  • 15 filters: remote, salary, level, languages, skills, visa, and more
  • Salary on every job (real data + smart estimates)
  • CV matching: upload your CV, sort by fit
  • Desktop alerts for new matching jobs
  • Auto-refresh in the background
  • Update, clear data, and uninstall from the Settings tab
  • 100% local. Zero telemetry. MIT licensed.
