Where does applying actually happen? Every job's apply link is classified by what sits behind it: a structured ATS form (Greenhouse, Lever, …), a job board (you apply on the board itself — sometimes with a board account, like Wuzzuf — or its listing points on to the real form), or the company's own site (an arbitrary web form). Pick your targets by effort.
